Tungelligence Getting Started Knowledge Base

Company leave policy:
Employees receive 20 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.
Leave requests must be submitted through the employee portal.
Managers are responsible for approving leave requests.

Security policy:
Passwords must be changed every 90 days.
Employees must not share passwords with other people.

Product information:
Tungelligence is a local-first knowledge assistant that works with workspace documents.
